About Us  

Elearningpathshala is a Platform Made by Dr. Devender Arya (Founder) and his team members from IIT's and many best universities across the world. When the entire world was suffering from COVID-19 Pandemic, and schools were closed, institutions, business etc. everything was closed and the whole economy was dropped down and, many of us were struggling for education, income, settlement etc. but there was no single solution. Seeing this, we did a hard research, and came up with an idea of one full-fledged app which can help students learn, and help parents to earn money also..

Elearning Pathshala is an Online / Offline Education Platform to provide well researched digital study material to students at their doorsteps so that they have a clear understanding of all the basic concepts.

 

 

#1 Conclusion

Covid-19 Pandemic has stopped the economy for the whole world by apporax 80%. But we believe that learning as well as earning never should be stopped.
